A free alternative to Microsoft Office

PulseOffice is a free, open-source office suite with a word processor, a spreadsheet and a presentation app. It opens and saves the same files as Microsoft Office, runs on Linux, Windows and macOS, and needs no subscription or account.

What you get

  • Folio, a word processor: styles, sections and columns, tracked changes and comments, tables of contents, footnotes, mail merge and right-to-left scripts.
  • Ledger, a spreadsheet: all 432 functions of the reference spreadsheets, pivot tables, conditional formatting, charts, sparklines and external data from ODBC, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQLite.
  • Deck, a presentation app: themes, masters and layouts, animations, a presenter view and export to video.

Honest limits

PulseOffice is still in development. Complex documents can look slightly different from Microsoft Office, and some features are new. When a file format cannot hold something you made, PulseOffice tells you before saving instead of dropping it silently. Keep backups of important documents and report problems on GitHub.
